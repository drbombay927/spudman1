## 1. Vault Gap Analysis

### 💪 Current Strengths
1. **Proven single-unit playbook** — Riverton runs on documented SOPs, a real training program, and a menu with identified heroes (Fully Stuffed Spud, Brisket Rancher, Buffalo Ranch Chicken).
2. **Clear brand identity** — "Loaded the Cowboy Way" is consistent across signage, voice, and menu naming. Most restaurants never get this.
3. **Organized vault structure** — numbered folders (00–05 + Spud Man 2) with a Spud Man HQ MOC means new docs have an obvious home.
4. **Financial visibility** — you track gross monthly and think in food-cost/margin terms, which puts you ahead of most 1-unit operators.
5. **Lean/zero-waste operating philosophy** — already a stated value, which makes the profitability and prep-planning docs below easy to enforce.
6. **Cookbook as a brand asset in motion** — "Spud Man Loaded" gives you a marketing flywheel and a forcing function to standardize recipes.

### 🕳️ Priority Gaps (ranked for Casper readiness)
| # | Gap | Why it bites |
|---|-----|--------------|
| 1 | **No single Casper launch countdown doc** | Planning notes exist, but with ~6 weeks to opening you need ONE master timeline with owners, deadlines, and go/no-go gates. Scattered notes = missed permit dates. |
| 2 | **Recipes aren't yet "clone-grade"** | Riverton recipes live partly in heads. Casper cooks who've never met you must produce an identical Brisket Rancher. Every recipe needs exact yields, batch sizes, hold times, and photos. |
| 3 | **Training program isn't location-portable** | The current program assumes you're in the building. Casper needs a version a designated trainer can run without you, with sign-off checklists. |
| 4 | **No mall-specific operations doc** | Eastridge means mall hours, food-court rules, shared trash/grease, mall marketing requirements, different rush patterns (weekend mall traffic vs. Riverton lunch). Flaming Wok's old space also means equipment conversion questions. |
| 5 | **Menu profitability is informal** | You know your winners by feel; you don't have plate-cost cards for every item, so Casper pricing (mall rent is higher!) is guesswork. |
| 6 | **No manager/keyholder layer documented** | Two locations = you can't be in both. Opening/closing authority, cash handling, ordering authority, and escalation rules need to be on paper for a Casper GM. |
| 7 | **Supply chain is single-location** | Vendor list, order guides, and par levels are Riverton-tuned. Casper needs its own par sheets plus a decision: shared commissary prep (brisket?) vs. independent ordering. |
| 8 | **Cookbook lacks a locked structure** | Recipes drift between "restaurant spec" and "home cook" versions. One template that generates both keeps the cookbook moving without stealing launch focus. |

**Descriptions:**

1. **Casper Launch Master Plan** (`06-Spud Man 2 - Casper/Casper Launch Master Plan.md`) — The single source of truth for the launch: week-by-week countdown from today to opening day, six workstreams (buildout, permits, hiring, supply, marketing, systems), go/no-go gates, and a soft-opening plan. If it's not in this doc, it doesn't exist.

2. **5-Day Staff Training Program v2** (`05-People/5-Day Staff Training Program v2.md`) — The existing program rebuilt to be *trainer-portable*: day-by-day lesson plans, skill sign-off checklists, and the Ranch Hand → Wrangler → Trail Boss progression, so a designated trainer can certify a Casper crew to Riverton standard without you in the building every day.

3. **Menu Profitability Framework** (`02-Menu & Recipes/Menu Profitability Framework.md`) — Plate-cost card template, target margin tiers, a menu engineering matrix in Spud Man language (Stallions / Workhorses / Wild Cards / Strays), waste log tie-in, and Casper-specific pricing rules that account for mall rent. Quarterly review cadence built in.

4. **Casper GM Playbook** — Everything a keyholder needs to run the store when you're 120 miles away: opening/closing authority, cash drops, ordering limits, who-to-call escalation ladder, daily manager checklist, and weekly reporting format back to HQ. This is the doc that makes two locations survivable.

5. **Master Recipe Card Template + rebuilds** — One template per recipe with restaurant spec (batch yield, station, hold time, plate cost link) and a cookbook-spec section (home quantities, story blurb). Rebuild Fully Stuffed Spud, Brisket Rancher, and Buffalo Ranch Chicken first as the gold standards; the rest follow the pattern.

6. **Eastridge Mall Operations Addendum** — Mall-specific SOP deltas: operating hours tied to mall hours, food-court cleaning/trash/grease rules, deliveries through mall receiving, mall marketing obligations, and the Flaming Wok equipment conversion punch list. Keeps the core SOPs universal and puts location quirks in one addendum.

7. **Two-Location Supply Chain & Par Sheet System** — Vendor master list with Casper delivery availability, per-location par sheets, and the commissary decision memo (does Riverton smoke brisket for both, or does Casper run independent?). Includes a weekly order calendar per location.

8. **Cookbook Structure v2** — Locked table of contents for "Spud Man Loaded" (chapters, recipe count per chapter, story sidebars), the recipe template (shared with #5), and a realistic post-launch production schedule so the book advances without stealing Casper bandwidth.

## 3. Navigation & Linking Improvements

### Spud Man HQ (Master MOC) upgrades
- **Add a "🔥 Right Now" block at the very top** — 3–5 links max to what matters *this month*: [[Casper Launch Master Plan]], hiring tracker, this review. Rotate it monthly. MOCs die when everything is equally important.
- **Add a per-location split**: `## 🏔️ Riverton` and `## 🛒 Casper` sections, each linking to that location's ops docs, so shared docs (recipes, brand) stay global and location docs stay separated.
- **Dashboard-ify with Dataview (if you use it):** a query listing all notes tagged `#task/open` or `status: 🔜` gives you a live to-do view without maintaining lists by hand.

### Cross-linking conventions
- **Every recipe links three ways:** its plate-cost card in [[Menu Profitability Framework]], its training module in [[5-Day Staff Training Program v2]], and its cookbook entry. When a recipe changes, you follow the links and update all three — that's how you prevent drift between store, training, and book.
- **Every SOP gets a `location:` frontmatter field** (`both` / `riverton` / `casper`) so you can filter and so the Casper GM Playbook can embed only what applies.
- **Frontmatter standard for all new docs:**
  ```yaml
  ---
  type: sop | plan | recipe | training | review
  location: both | riverton | casper
  status: draft | active | archived
  updated: 2026-07-10
  ---
  ```
- **Create a tiny "Casper Launch" MOC** inside 06-Spud Man 2 that just lists the launch docs in order of use. When the store opens, archive it and fold survivors into the main structure.
- **Weekly review ritual:** 15 minutes every Sunday — open Spud Man HQ, update the "Right Now" block, check the Master Plan countdown, log the week's numbers. The vault only compounds if it's touched weekly.
