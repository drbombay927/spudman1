# 🤠 5-Day Staff Training Program v2
*From greenhorn to trail-ready in five days — the Spud Man way.*

> [!info] What changed in v2
> This version is **trainer-portable**: any certified Trail Boss can run it at any location without Jim in the building. Every module has a lesson block, a hands-on block, and a **sign-off checkbox**. Nobody works a solo station until their checklist is signed.

## The Ranks
| Rank | Means | Earned by |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 🌱 **Ranch Hand** | New hire, in training | Day 1 |
| 🐎 **Wrangler** | Certified on all stations, works solo | Completing Day 5 sign-offs |
| ⭐ **Trail Boss** | Can train others, keyholder-track | 90 days + GM sign-off + runs one full training day solo |

**Trainer rule:** every location must always have at least one Trail Boss on staff. The trainer signs every checklist item personally — no batch sign-offs.

---

## Day 1 — The Brand & The Potato 🥔
*"You can't load a spud you don't respect."*

**Morning (classroom, ~2 hrs)**
- Welcome + Spud Man story: Riverton, the cowboy way, where we're headed
- Brand voice: how we talk to guests (friendly, western, genuine — never scripted-corporate)
- Tour: stations, storage, office, safety equipment
- Paperwork, uniform, schedule app

**Midday (safety, ~2 hrs)**
- Food safety fundamentals: temps (the Danger Zone), handwashing standard, glove rules, allergen basics (dairy, gluten in some toppings — know the map)
- Knife safety + cut-glove rule
- Cleaning chemicals: what, where, never-mix rules

**Afternoon (the potato, ~3 hrs)**
- The Spud Man bake: sizing, washing, oiling/salting spec, oven loading pattern, bake time/temp, doneness test, hold procedure & max hold time
- Hands-on: trainee runs one full oven load start to finish
- Waste philosophy: every potato costs money — track, don't trash; end-of-day potato count

**Day 1 Sign-offs**
- [ ] Handwashing demo passed
- [ ] Temp danger zone quiz (verbal, 100%)
- [ ] Ran one full bake cycle correctly
- [ ] Can state hold time limit and doneness test

## Day 2 — Prep & Stations 🔪
*"The rush is won at 9 AM."*

**Morning (~4 hrs)**
- Full prep list walkthrough: every topping, batch sizes, container/label/date standard
- Hands-on rotation: cheese sauce, brisket handling (slice/portion spec), buffalo chicken batch, bacon, green onion, sour cream portioning
- FIFO in walk-in and line — physically rotate stock

**Afternoon (~4 hrs)**
- Line setup: station diagram (identical at every location — learn once, work anywhere)
- Par levels: read the par sheet, stock the line to par
- Closing prep: what gets saved/labeled vs. counted as waste; waste log entry practice

**Day 2 Sign-offs**
- [ ] Produced 3 prep items to spec (trainer tastes/checks each)
- [ ] Labeled & dated everything correctly, unprompted
- [ ] Set the line to par from memory of the diagram
- [ ] Completed a waste log entry correctly

---

## Day 3 — The Build 🏗️
*"Every spud leaves here loaded like we mean it."*

**All day: build certification, item by item**
- The build order rule (potato → split → fluff → base → protein → sauce → toppings → finish) and why it matters (structure, portion, photo-worthiness)
- Portion discipline: scoops/ladles/scales per the recipe card — **portioning is the profit margin** (see [[Menu Profitability Framework]])
- Certify on each core item, trainer checks against the recipe card photo:
  - [ ] Fully Stuffed Spud
  - [ ] Brisket Rancher
  - [ ] Buffalo Ranch Chicken
  - [ ] *(remaining menu items — list per current menu)*
- Special requests & allergy builds: mod protocol, dedicated utensils
- Speed baseline: 10 correct builds, timed (target set by trainer; accuracy first, speed follows)

**Day 3 Sign-offs**
- [ ] Every core item built to photo standard
- [ ] Portion check: 5 random builds weighed, all within tolerance
- [ ] Allergy-mod build done correctly

---

## Day 4 — Service, POS & Speed 💨
*"Friendly and fast — in that order, but barely."*

**Morning (~3 hrs)**
- POS: order entry, mods, comps/voids (who authorizes), card/cash handling, drawer count
- Guest service the cowboy way: greet everyone, learn regulars' names, handle a complaint (Listen → Apologize → Fix it fast → Flag the lead)
- Mall-specific (Casper): food court dynamics, mall employee regulars, tray/napkin station upkeep

**Afternoon (~4 hrs)**
- **Mock rush:** trainer + off-shift staff fire 20+ orders in 30 minutes; trainee rotates through order-taking, building, expo
- Rush recovery: what to do when the hold cabinet runs empty (the bake-ahead call is the shift lead's most important decision)
- Between-rush duties: the "full hands, clean hands" standard — nobody stands idle, nobody touches food after cleaning without a wash

**Day 4 Sign-offs**
- [ ] POS transaction set completed (order, mod, void w/ authorization, cash & card)
- [ ] Mock rush at station without trainer intervention
- [ ] Complaint role-play passed

---

## Day 5 — Certification Shift ⭐
*"Ride the whole trail solo — we're right behind you."*

- Trainee works a **real full shift** at rotating stations; trainer shadows but does not touch
- Opening OR closing checklist executed by trainee, trainer verifying each line
- End-of-shift: full checklist review, gaps re-trained on the spot or scheduled
- **Wrangler ceremony** (yes, really): crew gathers, GM presents name tag / hat pin. Culture is built in these two minutes.

**Final Certification — Wrangler 🐎**
- [ ] All Day 1–4 sign-offs complete
- [ ] Full shift worked to standard, unassisted
- [ ] Opening or closing checklist executed correctly
- [ ] Food handler card obtained (or scheduled within 14 days per county rules)
- Trainer signature: ____________  Date: ______
- GM signature: ____________  Date: ______

## Running This Program for a New-Location Opening (Casper Mode)
1. Train the **GM/lead first** — embedded 1–2 weeks at Riverton before crew training week.
2. Run Days 1–5 **in the new space** with the whole opening crew as a cohort — it doubles as equipment shakedown.
3. Day 5 becomes the **mock service** (Go/No-Go Gate 3 in the [[Casper Launch Master Plan]]).
4. Riverton veterans work opening week as floating Trail Bosses.
