Hometown We Call Home

Welcome to Riverton's Favorite Spud Shop

Spud Man is a locally owned and operated business in the heart of Riverton, Wyoming. We believe in good food, large portions, and treating our customers like neighbors.

We have moved! Come see us at our new, larger location on Main Street to serve you better. We can't wait to see you there!

New Address: 702 E. Main St., Riverton, WY 82501

Phone: 307-463-5870

Meet James Brady, the Spud Man

James Brady, the owner and operator of Spud Man, is a Le Cordon Bleu graduate with over 20 years of culinary experience. He honed his skills in Las Vegas training under three James Beard Award-winning chefs, including the legendary Emeril Lagasse at Delmonico Steakhouse.

As the former owner of the Buffalo Jump Steakhouse and now Spud Man, James is dedicated to transforming the simple potato into a gourmet experience. He focuses on creative flavor combinations and sourcing the best local ingredients.
