Hometown We Call Home

Our Story

Spud Man is a locally owned and operated business in the heart of Riverton, Wyoming. We believe in good food, large portions, and treating our customers like neighbors.

Come find us on Main Street — we are proud to call Riverton home and can’t wait to serve you.

Address: 702 E. Main St., Riverton, WY 82501

Phone: 307-463-5870

Meet James Brady, the Spud Man

James Brady, the owner and operator of Spud Man, is a Le Cordon Bleu graduate with over 20 years of culinary experience. He honed his skills in Las Vegas training under three James Beard Award-winning chefs, including the legendary Emeril Lagasse at Delmonico Steakhouse. James brings his extensive background in fine dining and steakhouse cuisine to Riverton, focusing on exacting standards and exceptional food quality.

As the former owner of the Buffalo Jump Steakhouse and now Spud Man, James is dedicated to transforming the simple potato into a gourmet experience. He focuses on creative flavor combinations and sourcing the best local ingredients. James invites you to come in and taste the difference that passion and expertise make in every bite.
