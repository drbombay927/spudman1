## The Six Workstreams

### 1. 🔨 Buildout & Equipment (former Flaming Wok conversion)
- [ ] Walk the space with a punch list: hood system condition, grease trap, floor drains, electrical capacity, water heater
- [ ] Inventory what Flaming Wok left behind — what's usable (hood, sinks, refrigeration?) vs. haul-out
- [ ] **Potato-specific equipment order** (longest lead items FIRST — order this week if not done):
  - [ ] Convection/deck ovens sized for potato volume (know your Riverton peak-hour bake count; size Casper for mall weekend traffic)
  - [ ] Hot-holding cabinet(s) for baked inventory
  - [ ] Steam table / topping wells (match Riverton line layout so training transfers 1:1)
  - [ ] Refrigeration: reach-ins + prep table
  - [ ] Smallwares: full duplicate of Riverton's line kit (same tools = same training)
- [ ] Signage: mall façade sign (requires **mall landlord approval** — submit design NOW, approvals are slow), menu boards
- [ ] **Design rule:** make the Casper line a copy of Riverton's station layout wherever the space allows. Every difference is a training cost forever.

### 2. 📋 Permits & Compliance
- [ ] Casper-Natrona County Health Department: plan review → pre-opening inspection → permit to operate (*call them this week for current lead times — this is the #1 schedule risk*)
- [ ] City of Casper business license
- [ ] Wyoming sales tax license for the new location
- [ ] Food manager certification for at least one Casper-based person (not just Jim)
- [ ] Insurance: add location to GL/property/workers' comp; mall certificate-of-insurance requirements per lease
- [ ] Fire inspection / hood suppression certification (mall + city)
- [ ] Review lease exhibits for mall rules: hours of operation clause, percentage rent reporting, marketing fund obligations

### 3. 🤠 Hiring & Training
- [ ] Define the opening roster: 1 GM/shift lead + ~6–8 crew for mall hours, 7 days (Riverton runs Mon–Fri 11–6 / Sat 11:30–3 — Eastridge will demand roughly *double* the weekly service hours; staff and budget for it)
- [ ] Post openings NOW (Indeed, Facebook local groups, mall job board, "Now Hiring — Get Loaded the Cowboy Way" window sign)
- [ ] Interviews weeks of July 13–27; hire by ~Aug 1
- [ ] **Secret weapon:** bring the Casper GM/lead to Riverton for 1–2 weeks of embedded work *before* training week — they learn the culture at the source
- [ ] Run [[5-Day Staff Training Program v2]] in the Casper space the week before soft open (real equipment, real stations)
- [ ] One or two Riverton veterans on loan for opening week (schedule + pay bump agreed in advance)

### 4. 🥔 Supply Chain
- [ ] Confirm Sysco and US Foods deliver to Eastridge (existing accounts on [[Vendor Master List]]; mall receiving dock rules: delivery windows, dock access)
- [ ] Build Casper par sheets — start at ~120% of Riverton equivalents for opening surge, tune weekly
- [ ] **Decide the brisket question:** Riverton commissary smokes for both (food consistency, but a 120-mile cold chain) vs. Casper smokes its own (equipment + training). *Decide by mid-July; it drives equipment orders.*
- [ ] Potato supplier: confirm volume pricing at two-location scale — this is your #1 ingredient, renegotiate with the new volume
- [ ] Opening inventory order placed 2 weeks before soft open; delivered 4–5 days before

### 5. 📣 Marketing & Grand Opening
- [ ] Announce on all channels: "Spud Man is ridin' into Casper" — teaser now, date announcement once permits clear
- [ ] Mall marketing: get into Eastridge's socials/newsletter/directory (usually free — ask the mall marketing office)
- [ ] Grand opening offer: e.g., first 100 customers get a free Fully Stuffed Spud upgrade — cheap, drives a line, and *a line in a food court is its own advertisement*
- [ ] Local press: Casper Star-Tribune, Oil City News, local radio — "Riverton's loaded-potato favorite comes to Casper" is an easy local-business story
- [ ] Soft open (Friends & Family + mall employees): 2–4 days before grand opening; mall employees are your future regulars — feed them first
- [ ] Photograph everything during buildout for the launch story arc (also cookbook material)

### 6. 💻 Systems & Money
- [ ] POS: same platform as Riverton, new location profile; menu + pricing loaded per [[Menu Profitability Framework]] (mall pricing ≠ Riverton pricing)
- [ ] Bank account / bookkeeping: location-level P&L from day one — never mix the stores' numbers
- [ ] Payroll: add location, confirm any differing local requirements
- [ ] Set Casper's opening budget + a **contingency line of 15–20%** of buildout cost (mall conversions always find surprises behind the walls)
- [ ] Define week-one reporting: daily sales text to Jim, weekly P&L snapshot

## ⚠️ Top 5 Risks
1. **Health permit timing** — mitigate: call this week, submit everything at once, be the easy applicant
2. **Equipment lead times** — mitigate: order now, identify used/rental fallbacks for long-lead items
3. **Jim spread too thin across two towns** — mitigate: Casper GM empowered early (see GM Playbook, next doc up), Riverton runs on its own SOPs for 2 weeks as a stress test
4. **Mall traffic pattern surprises** (weekend-heavy vs. Riverton lunch-heavy) — mitigate: flexible scheduling first month, daily sales-by-hour review
5. **Quality drift at location 2** — mitigate: identical station layout, clone-grade recipe cards, Riverton veterans on loan
